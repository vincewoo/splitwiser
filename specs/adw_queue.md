# ADW queue

Briefs the `/adw-solo` or `/adw-orch` loop will work, in order. One line each:

    - [ ] specs/tasks/NN-slug.md — target: path/to/file.ext

Only briefs listed here are in scope. `specs/tasks/` also holds completed historical
briefs; the loop must never pick those up by scanning the directory.
